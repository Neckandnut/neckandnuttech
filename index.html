About Neck and Nut Tech

Founded by Joseph Jasef, a musician, music therapist, and gear enthusiast, Neck and Nut Tech was born out of a deep understanding of how the feel of an instrument can make or break a performance.

We're not just fixing problems — we're building confidence, vibe, and clarity into your playing experience.

Vision

To be the leading name in guitar tech services in Nigeria and beyond — trusted by artists, bands, schools, studios, and producers alike for reliable, artistic, and tech-savvy support.

Mission

To provide top-tier guitar setup and tech support services that elevate every player’s performance — from the first strum to the final encore.

What We Offer

  • Guitar & Gear Services: Full setup, intonation, string replacement, pickup wiring, bridge/nut repair, and mods
  • Studio & Stage Tech Support: On-stage emergency fixes, gear setup, pedalboard optimization
  • Provision of Gear: Guitars, pedals, cables, stands, transmitters (on request)
  • School Instrument Support: Maintenance, repairs, and setup for music departments

Who We Serve

  • Gigging Musicians
  • Bands
  • Music Studios
  • Churches & Event Organizers
  • Session Guitarists
  • Music Schools & Departments
  • Aspiring Guitarists

Our Payment Plans

2. Concert & Event Tech Plan

3. School Instrument Care Plan

Best for: Primary/Secondary schools, academies. Includes scheduled visits, emergency fixes, and maintenance guides.

4. Studio Maintenance Subscription

Best for: Studios and jam spaces. Includes 2 monthly visits, cable checks, minor repairs.

Note: Logistics fee varies by location and is confirmed during booking. No hidden charges.
